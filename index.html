Rooted in Jalingo. Growing beyond it.

Visionary Impact Initiative is a youth-focused organisation that began in Jalingo, Nigeria, where our first programmes took shape. We believe the next generation does not need to wait for permission to solve problems. With the right skills, support and platform, young people can earn, create and lead.

Jalingo is where we began, not where we stop. Our work brings education, digital skills, innovation and leadership development together in one pathway, and we are building it to reach young people in more communities, schools and states.
